Hotspot Computation Methodology & Mathematical Basis

The Persistence Index computes the frequency proportion of valid calendar days where PM2.5 concentrations surpass the National Ambient Air Quality Standard (60 µg/m³ 24h baseline). Acute risk envelopes are derived using empirical 90th percentile rankings calculated strictly over high-completeness records (>85% valid temporal capture). This intelligence matrix distinguishes localized point-source plumes from regional meteorology-driven inversions across the Indo-Gangetic Plains.
