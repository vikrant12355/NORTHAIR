## 2. Long-Term Multi-Year Observations

1. **Winter Season Stagnation**: Consistently across 2017–2023, November and December experience the highest frequency of Severe ($>400$) and Very Poor ($301-400$) AQI hours across Haryana, Punjab, and Chandigarh.
2. **Station Identity Safeguard**: Prior to station disambiguation, out-of-region stations (e.g. Kanpur, Jaipur) distorted Haryana historical metrics. With canonical reconciliation, state trends reflect true geographical airsheds.
3. **Network Growth**: Station coverage expanded substantially from 2017 (initial stations) to 2023, reflecting continuous network densification under the National Clean Air Programme (NCAP).
